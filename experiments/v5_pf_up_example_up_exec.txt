Select assay_chembl_id, target_type, tax_id, canonical_smiles, molecule_chembl_id, standard_type, and pchembl_value for activity rows against target CHEMBL260 (MAPK14). Join activities to assays for assay_chembl_id, to target_dictionary for target_type and tax_id, to molecule_dictionary for molecule_chembl_id, and to compound_structures for canonical_smiles. Filter to target_type SINGLE PROTEIN, tax_id 9606, standard_type IC50, standard_relation '=', and non-null pchembl_value. Preserve row multiplicity without DISTINCT. Order by molecule_chembl_id then assay_chembl_id.
