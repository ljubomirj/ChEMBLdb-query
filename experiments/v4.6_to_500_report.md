## Design Choice

The expansion deliberately stayed on the `target_pchembl` family for this push because:
- the family promoted cleanly under realistic-UQ rewriting
- capped first-1000 exports materialize quickly
- the current remaining baseline failures are concentrated in `assay_exact`, so bulk-adding more assay cases right now would expand the weakest family before redesigning it
