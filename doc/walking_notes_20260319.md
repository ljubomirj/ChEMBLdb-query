## Search vs Optimization

**Search algorithm:** Goes after discovering new interesting Qualities

**Optimization algorithm:** Goes after discovering right Quantities (of fixed qualities) that maximize

**Key insight:** `y = f(x)` in the vector (x, y) - don't assume only y is special; treat all of (x, y) as special. Find the best not-z for anyone z in (x, y).

**Goal:** Interesting AND new
